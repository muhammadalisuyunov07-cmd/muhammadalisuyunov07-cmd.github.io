Data Provenance & Terms of Use

Stock market data presented on this dashboard is strictly synthetic sample data stored locally in data/stocks.json. It does not reflect real-time pricing from KRX, Naver, or any securities exchange.

Foreign currency exchange rates are retrieved from Frankfurter, an open API delivering daily reference rates published by the European Central Bank (ECB). Rates are cached in browser localStorage for 60 minutes to minimize server load.

This site operates with a strict Content Security Policy and zero analytics or trackers.
